Solutions

Technology services shaped around your operations

Six areas of work, each aimed at a specific kind of business problem. Most projects combine more than one.

Custom Software Development

The problem
Off-the-shelf tools make a business bend its process around the software. The gaps get filled with spreadsheets, manual steps and workarounds that only one person understands.
What we do
We scope the problem with you, then build software around how your business works. We deliver in stages, document what we build, and design it so it can be extended later.
The value
Tools that fit the work, a result you own, and software that can change when the business does.

Website Design and Development

The problem
A website that is slow, unclear or hard to update loses people before they get in touch, and it often falls out of date because changing it is a chore.
What we do
We design and build responsive, accessible websites with a clear structure and straightforward content. We set them up so your team can edit what needs editing.
The value
A site that explains your business plainly, loads quickly on any device, and stays current.

Business Management Systems

The problem
Orders, customers, stock, invoices and schedules live in different places. Staff enter the same data twice, and reports from different tools do not agree.
What we do
We bring the functions you rely on into one system, with roles, permissions and reports that match the way your operation runs.
The value
One reliable source of information, less duplicated effort, and a clearer view of the business.

Workflow Automation

The problem
Copying data between tools, chasing approvals and sending routine messages take hours every week and invite small mistakes.
What we do
We map the process first, then automate the predictable steps. People stay in the loop wherever judgment is needed.
The value
Time returned to your team, fewer errors, and processes that run the same way each time.

AI-Powered Solutions

The problem
Many businesses see potential in AI but are unsure where it helps, where it adds risk, and how to use it without disrupting existing work.
What we do
We identify specific tasks where AI can help, such as sorting, summarising or searching information. We build it into your tools with review steps and clear limits.
The value
Practical use of AI where it earns its place, with human oversight and no unexplained black boxes.

Digital Transformation

The problem
Technology decisions made one tool at a time leave a business with systems that do not connect and no clear direction.
What we do
We review your current tools and processes, set priorities with you, and plan a sequence of changes your business can absorb.
The value
A roadmap grounded in your operations, so each investment supports the next one.

Not sure which one you need?

Describe the problem as you see it. We will help you work out where to start.
