Industries

Different businesses, different pressures

The challenges below are typical for each sector. We use them as a starting point, then adapt to your actual operation.

01 / 05

Retail and Commerce

Common challenges

  • Stock levels that differ between the shop, the website and the warehouse
  • Orders handled by hand across several channels
  • Little visibility into what sells and what sits

How technology helps

  • Inventory and order systems that keep channels in step
  • Online stores connected to back-office tools
  • Simple reporting on sales and stock
02 / 05

Restaurants and Hospitality

Common challenges

  • Bookings taken by phone, message and paper
  • Menus, suppliers and costs tracked in separate places
  • Rotas that take hours to plan and change

How technology helps

  • Reservation and ordering tools that fit your service
  • Supplier and stock tracking
  • Scheduling that reflects demand and availability
03 / 05

Professional Services

Common challenges

  • Time and billing managed in spreadsheets
  • Client information scattered across inboxes
  • Documents prepared by hand, again and again

How technology helps

  • Client portals and project tracking
  • Automated invoicing and payment reminders
  • Document workflows with approval steps
04 / 05

Small and Medium Businesses

Common challenges

  • Growing faster than the systems behind the business
  • Knowledge held by one or two people
  • Tools bought one at a time that do not work together

How technology helps

  • Right-sized systems that cover the essentials
  • Automation of routine admin
  • A website that carries the business and is easy to maintain
05 / 05

Other Business Sectors

Common challenges

  • Sector-specific routines, rules and reporting duties
  • Legacy tools that are difficult to replace
  • Processes that were never written down

How technology helps

  • A conversation first, to understand how your sector works
  • A scoped plan that builds only what you need
  • Software that can adapt as requirements change
